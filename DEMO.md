# NEXUS AI 2.0 — Live Evaluator Demo Guide

This guide details the evaluation pathways to experience the full emergency intelligence pipeline in under 5 minutes.

---

## 🌟 1. The Signature 17-Step Live Demo

1. Navigate to `/command` or click **"EXPLORE COMMAND CENTER"** from the landing page.
2. Look at the top navigation bar and click the glowing **"RUN LIVE DEMO"** button.
3. Observe the automated 17-step sequence executing in real-time:
   - System baseline status
   - Sensor anomaly detection (+1.8m surge)
   - Multi-stream AI verification matrix
   - Escalation of the NEXUS Risk Index to 88/100
   - Rendering of the hazard polygon on the GIS map
   - AI Emergency Commander generating 5 tactical directives
   - Automated unit assignment for Ambulance A-12 and NDRF Boat Squad
   - Evacuation corridor rendering (green safe routes vs. red blocked routes)
   - Completion modal: **"NEXUS RESPONSE COMPLETED"** with stabilized casualty metrics.

---

## 🎙️ 2. Multilingual Voice Assistant Demo

1. In the top navbar, click the **Microphone (Mic) icon**.
2. Select your dialect:
   - **English**
   - **Telugu (తెలుగు)**
   - **Hindi (हिंदी)**
   - **Tamil (தமிழ்)**
   - **Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)**
3. Click the Microphone button or type an emergency query (e.g. *"Where is the nearest shelter?"*).
4. Observe the live voice waveform visualizer, translated response, and spoken text-to-speech audio synthesis!

---

## 🌐 3. Offline Emergency Mode & Fault Tolerance

1. Click the **"NET: ONLINE"** button in the top navbar.
2. Watch the animated transition:
   `ONLINE` → `NETWORK LOST` → `[OFFLINE EMERGENCY MODE ACTIVE]`
3. Go to the **Citizen SOS** page (`/sos`), trigger a distress beacon.
4. Notice how the beacon is safely queued into local browser memory without crashing.
5. Click **"Restore Online"**: watch the status update to `SYNCING` and automatically flush queued reports to the central incident ledger.

---

## 🏢 4. 3D Emergency Digital Twin

1. Click **"3D Digital Twin"** in the sidebar (`/twin`).
2. Interact with the 3D WebGL scene:
   - Toggle **"Wireframe"** mode to inspect structural integrity.
   - Toggle **"Dynamic Pulse"** to visualize flood basin expansion and aerial drone flight paths.

---

## 👁️ 5. Computer Vision AI Lab

1. Click **"CV Vision Lab"** in the sidebar (`/computer-vision`).
2. Switch between sample feeds (Kurla Submersion, Nilgiris Wildfire, Expressway Tunnel Crash).
3. Click **"RUN INFERENCE"** and observe normalized bounding boxes with confidence scores.
4. Toggle bounding box visibility on/off.

---

## 📊 6. What-If Emergency Simulator

1. Click **"What-If Simulator"** (`/simulator`).
2. Adjust rainfall (mm/h), water depth, and road accessibility sliders.
3. Click **"RUN SIMULATION"** to execute the 8-stage dynamic progression and view before/after optimization impact tables.
